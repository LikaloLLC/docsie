How Docsie Works

Transform enterprise knowledge in three simple steps

Ingest

Turn training content into complete documentation. Automatically.

Your teams already produce training videos, demos, and onboarding sessions. Docsie converts this raw content into structured, high-quality documentation.

What happens:

  • Generate a full knowledge base structure automatically (topics, sections, articles)
  • Extract steps, workflows, screenshots, and explanations
  • Produce SOPs, user guides, and reference documentation
  • Consolidate knowledge scattered across wikis, LMS, and shared drives

Docsie uses multimodal AI + computer vision to understand your content and generate complete documentation — not transcripts, not notes.

Upload content → receive a structured knowledge base.

Manage

Your centralized knowledge base — versioned, reusable, always current.

Once generated, your documentation becomes a living, maintainable system for your entire organization.

What you can do:

  • Organize content into clear knowledge base topics and articles
  • Manage versions (v1 → v2 → client-specific variants)
  • Reuse content across projects, clients, and products
  • Translate automatically into core languages
  • Maintain structure, formatting, and style consistency
  • Collaborate with comments, presence indicators, and change history
  • Control access with role-based permissions

Docsie gives you one source of truth — built automatically, maintained effortlessly.

Learn More

Publish

Deliver secure, branded documentation portals — each tailored to the client or project.

  • Choose which content, versions, and languages each client sees
  • Publish dedicated portals with custom branding and domains
  • Connect each portal to the client's SSO / SAML / Azure AD / Google / Okta
  • Use JWT/OTP for embedded or internal delivery
  • Apply granular access rules and permissions
  • Manage thousands of portals from one dashboard
  • Keep all portals synced automatically with one central knowledge base

Create once. Deliver everywhere — securely, at scale.
