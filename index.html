How Docsie Works

From unstructured content to enterprise documentation in three simple steps.

🟧 CONVERT

Turn scattered content into structured documentation — automatically.

Docsie's AI agent transforms training videos, PDFs, and websites into clean, searchable docs. Start with one video, scale to your entire knowledge base.

  • Extract workflows and step-by-step guides from training videos
  • Convert manuals and scanned PDFs with AI vision
  • Import complete documentation sites at scale
  • Refine structure through conversational AI — preview, edit, approve
Powered by Multimodal AI & Computer Vision

🟦 MANAGE

Your documentation system — versioned, collaborative, always current.

Once content is generated, Docsie becomes the control center for everything: structure, versions, variants, translations, and team workflows.

  • Branch, diff, and merge versions — including client-specific variants
  • Define granular roles, permissions, and approval workflows
  • Translate into 20+ languages with automated AI workflows
  • Use reusable templates and structured content blocks
  • Track every change with reviews, history, and audit trails
Built for Version Control & Collaboration at Scale

🟩 DELIVER

Deploy secure, branded client portals — instantly.

From one knowledge base, publish thousands of isolated client portals with custom branding, routing, and access control.

  • White-label portals with custom domains, themes, and layouts
  • Smart access routing by email domain, SSO attributes, or rules
  • Enterprise SSO/SAML (Azure AD, Okta, Google) with granular roles
  • Secure invitations with OTP, magic links, and per-portal policies
  • Built-in analytics: search behavior, engagement, usage patterns
  • Manage every portal from a single workspace — at any scale
One Knowledge Base → Unlimited Client Portals
